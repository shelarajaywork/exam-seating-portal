2. Room Layouts (Bench Rows × Bench Columns)

Each bench seats 2 candidates, so capacity = Rows × Cols × 2. "Peak Candidates" is the largest number of candidates the schedule puts in that room at one time; capacity must cover it. If a room's columns have different numbers of rows, fill in Rows per Column (e.g. 6,6,5,5 = 4 columns, first two with 6 benches, last two with 5). That field overrides Rows/Cols for that room.
